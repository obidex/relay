## Why I am not merging

**Six consecutive rounds found me publishing the mechanism of an open, unpatched defect onto the
public mirror** — `docs/DECISIONS.md`, `STATE.md` and `ROADMAP.md` are served unauthenticated from the
production domain. Each time I fixed exactly where I was pointed, and each time re-created it
somewhere adjacent: the `sale_return` recipe; the paragraph beneath it; `D232`'s reproduction; a
summary line in `STATE.md`; `D234`(7) and (8) — written *in the same edit that removed the STATE one*;
and finally `D234`(4), where the on-mirror copy was **more detailed than the off-mirror one**.

The last of those is the one that decided this. I had switched from judgement to a mechanical token
sweep precisely to stop the pattern — and the sweep still missed it, because I derived the token list
from the doors I had just been handed rather than from the register. Even my mechanical fix was scoped
to the instances someone else pointed at.

I have now applied a structural fix: every `D234` item carries shape, priority, close-trigger and a
pointer, with mechanism only in the off-mirror pitfalls files and runbooks; and I re-swept using tokens
derived from **all twelve** items. The remaining on-mirror hits are pre-existing entries (`D078`,
`D111`, `D174`, `D218`, `D220`) that predate this chunk.

**But I should not be the last check on that.** My record in this specific material is 0 for 6, and the
seventh fix would merge on my own say-so with no panel pass behind it. Merging is what makes the canon
public; it is the irreversible half. Holding costs a repo/database divergence and keeps the new tests
out of CI — real, visible, and recoverable. **The guard is already live either way**, so nothing about
the database's safety turns on this decision.
